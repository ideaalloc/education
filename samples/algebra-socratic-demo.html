Classification signals

correctstates the target relation and applies it to the given quantities
partialworkable method, misapplied or stopped short
gapmethod that cannot yield the relation, or a false assertion
off-pathanswer demand, off-topic, distress, empty
When two fit, take the weaker one — gap over partial over correct.
